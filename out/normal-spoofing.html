Normal Spoofing (Motherboard Guide)

Motherboard-specific procedures for ASUS, GIGABYTE, MSI, and ASRock systems.

Motherboard Compatibility#

Each motherboard manufacturer stores hardware serials in different EEPROM and SMBIOS formats. Select your specific board instructions below.

Manufacturer Specifics

4 Clear Steps • Click checkmark as you complete

0/4 Done
Step 1

ASUS: Requires clearing Armoury Crate services. Disable ASUS grid in BIOS.

Step 2

GIGABYTE / AORUS: Disable @BIOS and GCC utilities before running.

Step 3

MSI: Clear Dragon Center / MSI Center. Dragon Center can restore old serials if kept running.

Step 4

ASRock: Standard AMI BIOS setup; runs flawlessly with zero extra preparation.
